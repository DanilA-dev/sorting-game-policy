Privacy Policy for Supermarket Chaos: Tidy Up

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how "Supermarket Chaos: Tidy Up" (the "Game"), developed by Danil Almuka ("I", "me", "developer"), handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information I collect

I do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location or any other personal information directly. The Game does not require an account.

2. Third-party advertising

The Game displays ads using the Yandex Mobile Ads SDK, a third-party service. The SDK may automatically collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system version, language, screen settings)
  • Information about ad interactions (impressions, clicks)

This data is used to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. It is collected and processed by Yandex under its own privacy policy: https://yandex.com/legal/privacy/

You can limit ad personalization at any time in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization).

3. Local data

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. I do not have access to this data. It is removed when you uninstall the Game.

4. Data sharing

I do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the advertising provider described above, to the extent necessary to display ads.

5. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact me and I will make sure any such information is deleted.

6. Data retention and deletion

I do not store personal data on my own servers. Data held by the advertising provider is subject to its retention policy. To request deletion of data associated with you, contact me at the email below and also use the provider's privacy tools.

7. Changes to this policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, contact me at: danil.almuka@gmail.com
